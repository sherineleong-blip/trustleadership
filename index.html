The TRUST Leadership Programme

"It's not about doing more. It's about trusting more."

New managers rarely struggle because they have too much to do — they struggle because they haven't learned to trust their team enough to let go. TRUST is a modular system that fixes the real problem, not the symptom. Clients enter at whatever stage matches their gap.

T

T1 — Self Leadership (2D1N)

The personal foundation — discover the gap through experiential team building.

R

T2 — Dynamic Leadership (3D2N)

Managers apply TRUST at the team level and confront where they're holding on too tight.

U

Retreat

Immersive, small-group work surfaces what day-to-day hierarchy keeps hidden.

S

1-1 Coaching

A private space to work through what surfaced, respecting power-distance dynamics.

T

TRUST (6-Month Programme)

The shift becomes culture — coached, measured, and sustained with Coaching Culture International.

T — Transform. Shift identity from doer to leader.

R — Release. Let go of control and delegate with confidence.

U — Unmask. Surface the blind spots hierarchy hides.

S — Safety. Build the psychological safety trust requires.

T — Transfer. Embed the shift into lasting practice.

Built to measure — not just deliver.

Most leadership training stops at a feedback form. TRUST is built to reach the levels most programmes never do — behaviour change and measured results.

Every engagement comes with three things in hand, not just a workshop:

1

A baseline score, before day one.

The TB Diagnostic measures the gap across all five TRUST shifts, so you know exactly where things stand before any training happens.

2

A blind-spot report.

Manager self-view compared against direct-report view, on the same behaviours — showing precisely where perception and reality have drifted, delivered privately.

3

A remeasured scorecard at the close of the 6-month stage.

Same instrument, same questions — so the change is shown, not assumed.

That's the same standard L&D uses to judge whether training actually works — Kirkpatrick's Level 3 and Level 4 — the two levels most programmes never reach, because they'd need exactly this kind of before-and-after measurement.
